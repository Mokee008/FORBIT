🔬 Why is this Tree Heartbeat Graph Here? Normal 3-Year Forest Cycle

Indian forest trees (Sal, Teak, Bamboo) naturally shed their leaves every year during dry months (February to April). Simple systems mistake this normal leaf-fall for deforestation, sending out thousands of false alarms. Just like an ECG monitors a human heart's rhythm, this graph tracks the natural seasonal pulse of the trees over 3 years so it only alerts when trees are actually cut down:

Green Band (Normal Season Range)

Safe area. Normal dry-season leaf drop stays inside this green band and is safely ignored as natural.

Cyan Dots (Clear Satellite Photos)

Sentinel-2 10m clear-sky camera photos tracking healthy green tree leaves month by month.

Grey Dots (Rainy Monsoon Clouds)

Cloudy days where cameras see only white clouds. Sentinel-1 radar takes over and sees right through the clouds.

Red Line & Dots (Canopy Destroyed)

True tree destruction. The signal crashes down to bare ground (~0.18) and never bounces back.
